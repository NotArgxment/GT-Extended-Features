# Features for Players

## Multiblocks

### **Robust Alloy Materializer [RAM]**
A bigger Alloy Blast Smelter that has Perfect Overclocks and Batching capabilities, but only allows 1 energy hatch

### **Large Cracking Machine [LCM]**
IV Version of the Cracker, may help with Oil-Based infrastructure

### **Large Pyrolysis Oven [LPO]**
IV Version of the Pyro Oven, allows parallel hatches

### **Synthesys Vessel [SyV]**
My "vision" of what most players know as **Chemical Plant**.

As of now, only performs shortened recipes for the Platline and Naqline, in future updates it will get recipes for Polymers + other recipes that could have a shortened variant 

### **Expanded Assembly Line [EAL]**
An assembly line that allows **Gregtech Stocking Hatchess (An AE2 Integration)** for both Item and Fluid input, but is limited to **Only One Energy Hatch** and **Wireless Optical Receptors**

### **Rock Processing Plant [RPP]**
Intended to simplify processing lines of Rocks combining a Macerator, an Electrolyzer and a Centrifuge all in one single multi
_(although it may need another structure rework for the centrifuge part lmao)_

### **Industrial Greenhouse [IG]**
What do i need to say here? it just allows an easier way to obtain wood

### **Artificial Conservatory [AC]**
The previous, but IV :v

### **Universal Dissasembly Machine [UDM]**
Indexes all crafting, assembler and assembly line recipes for machines/multiblock controllers, and allows the player to disassemble and get back all the components used to made it in the first place

### **Matrix Data Relay [MDR]**
Wireless data bank, needs 144mb/t of PCB Coolant while working.

Each part it allows drains a different EU value:
- The LuV Transmissor consumes 1A of ZPM Energy `(131.072 EU/t)`
- The ZPM Transmissor consumes 1A of UV Energy `(524.288 EU/t)`
- THe UV Transmissor consumes 1A of UHV Energy `(2.097.152 EU/t)`
- Regular Data Hatches uses `1980 EU/t`
- Expanded Data Hatches uses `4096 EU/t`

### **Large Gas Collector [LGC]**
Multiblock version of the regular Singleblock Gas Collectors

### **Power Transformer [PwT]**
Active transformer that can be expanded to 4 rows that allows any inputs or outputs

### **Large Fisher [LF]**
Allows gathering large quantities of fish
